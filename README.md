# THE FLYING DUTCHMAN

A monochrome, Game Boy-style adventure in four worlds.

- **World 1: Hollowhaven.** Explore a haunted island town, find the missing mayor, befriend one
  of five spirits, and summon The Flying Dutchman, the Cursed Captain of the Endless Sea, for a
  three-phase turn-based boss fight.
- **World 2: The Flooded Island.** Saltwash is being destroyed by impossible tsunamis. Help an
  eccentric inventor build a submarine, survive a rundown theme park, win an election, rob a
  motorcycle dealership, dive into the Cave of the Gods, solve one god's temple, and face the
  Kraken with two creatures at your side.
- **World 3: The Party Island.** Bacchus Key never stops partying, until someone starts
  sending steel Titans after its bartender, its booze barrel and its nightclub. Fake an ID,
  follow a thief home, and find out who is behind it.
- **World 4: The Forgotten Island.** An empty island, a black cat that knows the way, and two
  guardian spirits who no longer trust anyone. Climb the Woodlands Tower or fly a crashed
  airship into the giant tree, then run when something comes to collect.

Everything (pixel art, font, sound effects, music) is generated by code in this project.
There are no external assets, no backend and no network calls.

## Requirements

- [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) 18 or newer (includes `npm`)
- Chrome or Edge (any modern WebGL browser works)

## Install and play

```bash
cd flying-dutchman
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open the address Vite prints (by default **http://localhost:5173**).

### Build a static copy

```bash
npm run build
npm run preview
```

`dist/` is a plain static site. Because it uses relative paths, you can host it anywhere, or
serve it with any static file server.

## The story

The game starts in Hollowhaven's town square. The mayor is missing, the townsfolk are frightened,
and the Mayor's Office is locked. Talk to everyone, look into the well, and follow the trail.

- Once the office is open, five spirits can be found around town: on the **farm** (west), in
  the **mansion** (east), deep in the **sea cave** behind the park (west), at the **grocery
  store** (east) and at the **psychic's house** (north). You can only befriend **one**.
  Whoever you choose fights the Dutchman beside you.
- The psychic knows more than she lets on. The **dock** (south) has a bell that needs
  replacing, and the fishing shop has a rod for anyone who can bring the shopkeeper some bait.
- Fish from any shoreline or dock once you have a rod. Everything you catch goes in your bag.
- **Mad Pies**, at the east end of North Road, is an optional stop: Maddie's angry-faced pies,
  Morgan's phone, and Roxie, a dog with a pie habit and an old friend in town.
- If you lose the fisherman or the Dutchman, you come back where the fight started with all
  your progress. Try again whenever you are ready.
- Progress saves automatically in your browser. **NEW GAME** in the pause menu starts over.
- Once World 1 is finished, **RUSH** in the pause menu lets you fight the Dutchman again with
  any of the five spirits.

### World 2: The Flooded Island

When the mayor's office party ends, the adventure carries on to a new island, with the same
spirit at your side. (Saves that finished World 1 earlier get the invitation when loaded, or
from the mayor in his office.)

- Talk to the islanders, then find **Professor Pipwick**'s workshop on Low Street. He joins
  you, follows you everywhere, and gives hints if you talk to him.
- His **checklist** (pause menu) has four parts, collected in any order:
  - The **Synchronous Reciprocating Transducer**: test three rides at **Thrill Harbor**, the
    theme park at the top of Harbor Road.
  - The **Phase-Conjugate Disambiguated Deflector**: City Hall, the bank, an election, and the
    Tech Repair shop. Eight voters, five who can be convinced. Listen to what each one is
    angry about, and use what the mayor tells you. You can always retry a debate.
  - The **Turbo-Encabulator**: commonly found in motorcycles. Rick's Rides is on Center Avenue.
  - **Peanut butter**, last. The grocery store stays locked until the island is evacuated.
- Losing to **Slick Rick** puts you back in the grocery store with all your progress.
- **Luke & Grace's Thrift** on Shore Lane is optional. Everything is for sale. Nothing should be.
- In the **Cave of the Gods**, look at all four statues, then answer one. Only that god's door
  ever opens, so each playthrough solves exactly one temple:
  - **Zeus**: turn the conductor pillars until the lightning reaches the sealed door. Watch
    where it fizzles. Wins you **Glowbell**.
  - **Thor**: light three runes (a block on a plate, a block into a pit, an orb into a copper
    channel and a strike on the gong), then lift the hammer and break the cracked wall. Wins
    you **Hammerfin**.
  - **Poseidon**: the tide wheel floods the west, the sluice holds the east shut. Float the
    crate, let the rush carry it east, then drain it onto the plate. Wins you **Bubblotl**.
  - **Athena**: wake the four guardians in the order the library's verses give (one is behind
    a bookcase). A wrong order just resets them. Wins you **Glidray**.
  - Every temple has a shrine or statue that resets its puzzle.
- Every temple leads to the same quiet **vault**, and to **the Kraken**: a two-creature fight.
  Switching costs your turn, a knocked-out creature hands over automatically, and you only
  lose when both are down. Losing puts you back in the vault, at the edge of the pit.
- Then **run**: 60 seconds back to the submarine through collapsing, flooding tunnels. If time
  runs out, the escape (only the escape) starts again.
- The island you come back to is empty. Walk up from the dock with the professor.
- Saying goodbye to him sets sail for World 3. (Saves that finished World 2 earlier get the
  invitation when loaded, or from the professor.)

### World 3: The Party Island

Bacchus Key: Club Forever, the Great Barrel, and a lot of people who have not slept.

- **Day 1.** The bouncer wants ID. Avalena's art shop can help, for a price, and the price
  is a painting someone wants to buy at the barrel.
- **Night 1.** Enjoy the club (dance, the DJ, the bar, the hotel, the window, the clinic).
  After a few activities the back door unlocks. What follows is not a joke. Then **follow**
  someone home without being seen: keep a few tiles back, and stay well back when she looks
  over her shoulder. Too close or too far starts the follow again from the club.
- **Day 2.** Find Avalena, fill a jug at the Great Barrel, visit the clinic. Ayla joins you.
- **Day 3.** A quiet town. Visit the five businesses on Food Row, then tell Avalena which two
  are busiest.
- Three Titan fights: **Scorvex**, **Widowmaker** (three phases) and **Ironclad**, with its
  master on the field giving orders. Every Titan uses your main companion with the next one in
  reserve. Losing offers a rematch on the spot.
- At the end you take one of three young spirits and decide one person's future. Both choices
  lead to the credits.

### World 4: The Forgotten Island

Finishing World 3 brings the invitation. The island is silent and almost empty.

- **Act 1.** Follow the black cat up the mountain to the monument, then explore: the abandoned
  village, the Ancient Forest, the crashed airship, the castle. Read everything.
- **Act 2: choose a route by doing, not by menu.** Putting two gemstones in the Woodlands Tower
  door commits you to **Route A**; starting the airship commits you to **Route B**. Either one
  locks the other.
- **Route A, the Woodlands Tower (Wispmane).** Gems from the cat's trail (storage barn, second
  house, the well) and the airship captain's room. Seven floors: rotating vines, the **Ancient
  Forest Champion**, four thirsty stumps, a sleeping garden, the vine swing (A pumps, B lets
  go), three sacred stones, and an old man who feeds a lion salad. Then **Wispmane** in the
  clearing.
- **Route B, the airship (Viperwind).** Keys from the dark cabin, the wheel from Carlo in the
  castle (he joins), fuel from the **Mutant Crow** on the mast. A five-step start-up minigame,
  a flight, and seven floors of the giant tree downwards: a reunion, the **Ancient Tree
  Guardian**, the treehouse, the botanical chamber, the singing mushrooms (repeat Carlo's
  tune), a collapsing floor (run!) and the flute. Then **Viperwind** in the clearing.
- **Acts 4 to 7.** Back in the village square with your companion: the last warning, Flamora,
  and the Reaper. A timed escape (falling rocks and trees; it follows you), the airship, the
  sky, the volcano, and a fight against the Reaper. Then the cell. TO BE CONTINUED.
- Every World 4 boss uses all three of your creatures. Losing offers a rematch.

## Controls

### Exploring

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walk | Arrow keys (or WASD) | On-screen pad |
| Run | Hold Shift or X | Hold B |
| Speed up a cutscene | Hold Shift | – |
| Talk / examine / confirm (A) | Z, Enter or Space | A button, or tap the dialogue box |
| Cancel (B) | X or Backspace | B button |
| Pause menu (companion, bag, map, goal) | Esc, M or Tab | Start button |

### Battles

| Action | Keyboard | Mouse / touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move through menus | Arrow keys (or WASD) | Hover / tap |
| Confirm | Enter (or Z) | Click / tap a highlighted option |
| Back | Esc | – |
| Quick-pick an ability | 1–4 | – |
| Fast-forward animations | Hold Space | Hold anywhere on the screen |

On touch screens, the first tap on an ability shows its full description and the second tap uses it.

The bar under the screen has **sound on/off**, **volume**, **animation speed (1x–3x)**,
**reduced motion** (calmer camera, no screen inversion or strong flashes) and **fullscreen**.
Settings are remembered in your browser. Audio starts after your first click or key press.

## How the Dutchman fight works

- Every turn, the chip in the top-left shows the Dutchman's **next attack**: one pip per hit,
  the expected damage after your defences, and an icon for any curse it leaves behind. A
  blinking **!** means it is dangerous.
- Abilities cost **EN** (energy, shown as pips) and refill a little each turn. Stronger moves
  have **cooldowns** (hourglass), and each champion has a **signature** ability (marked `*`).
- **Charged attacks** take a turn to prepare. Deal enough damage before they fire to **BREAK**
  them, or brace yourself.
- The Dutchman does not stay the same for long. Watch the storm and learn its new tricks.

### The champions

| Champion | Class | HP | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BOOHEET | Debuffer | 340 | Keep WEAKENED up and stack HEX. Save PEEK-A-BOO for the biggest telegraphed hit. NIGHT TERROR deals more damage per HEX stack, and its HAUNT heals you. |
| SHIPWRAITH | Defense | 400 | Shield before heavy single blows and fire DEAD MAN VOLLEY into the biggest hits. The counter scales with the damage you prevent. |

## Balance testing

The combat engine (`src/engine`) is plain TypeScript with no Phaser dependency, so it can be
simulated headlessly:

```bash
npm run sim          # 2000 fights per creature (pass a number to change it)
npx tsx sim/sim.ts 300 fisherman   # the cellar fight instead of the Dutchman
npx tsx sim/sim.ts 300 salesman    # Slick Rick, World 2's mini-boss
npx tsx sim/sim.ts 300 kraken      # the Kraken, every World 1 spirit with every temple creature
npx tsx sim/sim.ts 150 ironclad w3 # a World 3 Titan (scorvex, widowmaker, ironclad)
npx tsx sim/trace.ts tickraith 3   # turn-by-turn log of one simulated fight
npx tsx sim/maps-check.ts          # every door, NPC and item on every map is reachable
```

The simulator plays each creature with a thoughtful rule-based player, a random player, and a
"basic attack only" player. With current numbers, the thoughtful player wins about 36–56% of fights
with every creature. Random play rarely wins, and spamming basic attacks never does.

## ROGUELIKE (1-4 player co-op)

A second mode, separate from the story and its save: ROGUELIKE on the title screen.

- **Solo** needs nothing extra: PLAY SOLO runs entirely inside the game.
- **Online** needs the multiplayer server, a small Node process that also serves the game:

  ```
  npm run host        # builds the game, then serves game + multiplayer on port 8787
  ```

  Everyone opens `http://<the host's address>:8787`, one player picks CREATE ROOM and shares the
  four-letter code, the others pick JOIN ROOM. On the same network that works as it is; over
  the internet the host has to forward port 8787 or run the server somewhere public (set `PORT`
  to change the port). While developing, `npm run dev` and `npm run server` side by side do the
  same job (the dev server passes multiplayer through to port 8787).

The server decides everything (rooms, fights, rewards); players only send requests. Code lives in
`src/rogue` (`shared/` is the rules and runs on both sides) and `server/server.ts`.

Tests and tuning, all headless:

```
npx tsx sim/rogue-net-test.ts   # four simulated players against the real server (needs `npm run server`)
npx tsx sim/rogue-net-full.ts   # four simulated players play a whole online run, start to summary
npx tsx sim/rogue-run.ts 20     # whole runs for 1-4 players, start to summary
npx tsx sim/rogue-sim.ts 40     # single rooms and bosses, to tune src/rogue/shared/config.ts
```

In a solo run, the backquote key (`) opens test shortcuts: jump to any room type or puzzle,
pick the floor's boss, start any floor, start any of the Grim Reaper's three fights.

### Hosting online multiplayer on Render

One Render **web service** serves the game and hosts the rooms, so players just open its URL.
`render.yaml` describes it (build: `npm ci --include=dev && npm run build`, start: `npm start`,
health check: `GET /health`). The server listens on Render's `PORT` and on `0.0.0.0`.

1. Put this folder in a GitHub (or GitLab) repository and push it.
2. On render.com: **New > Blueprint**, pick the repository, and apply. (By hand instead:
   **New > Web Service**, runtime Node, the build and start commands above, health check path
   `/health`.)
3. When the deploy is live, open `https://<service>.onrender.com`, choose ROGUELIKE, and create
   a room. `https://<service>.onrender.com/health` shows the open rooms and players.

Good to know:

- Rooms live in memory: a redeploy or restart ends runs in progress (players get the connection
  lost screen and can make a new room).
- On Render's free plan the service sleeps after 15 minutes with no visitors and takes about a
  minute to wake on the next visit. A paid instance stays awake.
- The game's server address lives in one place: `src/rogue/net/serverConfig.ts`
  (`ROGUE_SERVER`, now `wss://wispbound.onrender.com/rogue-ws`). Every copy of the game, wherever
  it is served from, connects there over a secure WebSocket. A build can override it with
  `VITE_ROGUE_SERVER` (see `.env.example`), and players can type another address in the lobby's
  SERVER field (e.g. `localhost:8787` to test a local server); that always wins.
- If the server is asleep, the lobby says it is waking it up and keeps trying for up to 75
  seconds before reporting it unavailable. PLAY SOLO never touches the network.
- To test the live server: `ROGUE_URL=wss://<service>.onrender.com/rogue-ws npx tsx sim/rogue-net-full.ts`.
